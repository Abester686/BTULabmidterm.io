A space for makers of all types.

The lab welcomes makers with different experiences and interests. Learn about access and find the people behind BTU.

Community

Who can use BTU?

Anyone can work toward gaining access to the BTU Lab. Its stated goal is to support a diverse, inclusive community of makers.

New members attend a general orientation before getting started.

Guest card note

Due to increased campus security, the BTU Lab says it is currently unable to sponsor BuffOne Guest cards.

Check directly with lab staff if you have access questions.
