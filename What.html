Materials policy

Materials are an exchange.

The original BTU site emphasizes that the lab is not a free supply store. Members who need materials are asked to give something back to the community.

Ways to contribute

  • Display work you made in the lab
  • Tag BTU in social media posts
  • Offer a workshop
  • Volunteer to organize supplies

These examples come from BTU's original materials policy.
