Wood shop safety

Safety matters.

  • Only use equipment you know how to operate; ask for assistance when unsure.
  • Do not work alone when you could be out of earshot of others.
  • Report damaged or malfunctioning tools to staff.
  • Major wood shop machines are powered off daily from 10 p.m. to 6 a.m.
